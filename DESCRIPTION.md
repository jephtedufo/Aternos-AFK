# M3GAN - Intelligent Minecraft Bot

M3GAN is an advanced, autonomous Minecraft bot that brings lifelike AI behavior to your server. Unlike simple AFK bots, M3GAN features sophisticated movement patterns, environmental awareness, and human-like interactions that make her feel like a real player. She wanders intelligently within a designated spawn area, switches between walking and sprinting naturally, and can even pause to observe her surroundings. M3GAN actively interacts with the environment by opening doors, pressing buttons, flipping levers, and checking chests—making her presence dynamic and engaging. When nearby players appear, she detects them and follows the nearest one for 30 seconds before returning to her own activities, creating organic social interactions. The bot also manages her own survival needs: when hunger gets low, she searches her inventory for food (cooked beef or steak), and if none is found, she autonomously navigates to a designated chest to retrieve food and eat it. To add even more personality, M3GAN randomly holds different items from her inventory and performs spontaneous actions like crouching for a few seconds or jumping playfully while running around. Built with robust error handling and automatic reconnection features, M3GAN is designed to run reliably on any platform, recovering gracefully from network interruptions or server disconnects without manual intervention.
